### It's a what now?

**SpeedFriending** is a feature of UniNexus available to student unions, clubs and societies, and faculties, schools, etc. organising orientation events. It provides live social features to support “speed networking” style events for
meeting classmates and forming groups of friends at uni.

### Why tho?

We know that students do better academically and enjoy uni more when they attend and engage with campus life and feel like they belong[^1], but attendance and engagement have declined in recent years. Students are getting and
staying involved on campus less than they intend to[^2], contributing to loneliness and a reduced sense of belonging in university communities[^3].

Many students’ experiences of developing friendships at university does not match their expectations; participants of one UK study expected the university to play a far greater role in supporting friendship formation, expected “frequent
socialising and an intensely social experience”[^4].

Orientation Week, Welcome Back Week, and similar events play a vital role in helping students establish their social networks and sense of belonging at uni. **SpeedFriending** gives students a place to come together and forge the
connections they’re looking for.
